We've served 700+ players. Everything happens privately on WhatsApp, and we walk you through changing your password and turning on 2FA after delivery.

Why are prices different during events?

When the game runs a double-CP event, you get twice the CP for the same price. Our list always shows the current deal and when it was last updated.

Do you deliver outside Kenya and Tanzania?

Ask us on WhatsApp. We'll tell you straight away if we can.

What if something goes wrong?

Message us. If we can't deliver, you get your money back.
